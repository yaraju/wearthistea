## Unmet-needs synthesis `[inference]`
The Gen-Z buyer who wants to *wear a cultural moment* today is forced to choose between: (a) **marketplaces** — cheap and searchable but saturated, generic, and frequently low-quality/slow with no human help; (b) **reactive brands** — closer in spirit but generic-trendy, thin on trust, and not personal; (c) **DIY/AI tools** — free but produce generic, text-broken art and still require fulfillment setup. **No option combines: sharp gossip-native voice + personalization + curated quality + human-checked safety + speed.** That intersection is the opening.

## Customer-discovery questions (for a real 02 pass)
1. Last time something blew up online and you wanted to react — would you have *worn* it? What stopped you?
2. Where do you currently buy graphic/meme tees, and what's gone wrong (quality, shipping, support)?
3. Would "a design only you have, made from your tea" be worth more than a $15 marketplace tee? How much more?
4. How fast does a drop have to ship before the joke feels stale to you?
5. What would make you *trust* a brand-new gossip merch brand enough to buy?
6. Which matters more: that it's funny/sharp, that it's original/yours, or that it's cheap?
7. Would you share/post the tee? What would make it post-worthy?
